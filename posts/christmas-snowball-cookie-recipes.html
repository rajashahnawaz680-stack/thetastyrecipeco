Snowball cookies are the quiet stars of every Christmas cookie tray: buttery, nutty, melt-in-your-mouth rounds rolled twice in powdered sugar until they look like fresh snow. This roundup gathers twenty-five Christmas snowball cookie recipes — classic pecan and walnut, chocolate and mocha, peppermint and coconut, fruit and spice, and a few special flavors like matcha and salted caramel. Bake one batch for the cookie exchange, freeze a second for Christmas Day, and keep a jar of powdered sugar handy — the second roll is what makes them snowballs.

How to bake perfect snowball cookies

Chop the nuts fine so the dough holds together and every bite has crunch. Roll the warm cookies in powdered sugar as soon as they come out of the oven, then roll them a second time once they are cool — the first coat melts into the cookie and the second stays snowy white. Bake just until the bottoms are set; snowballs should stay pale, not brown. Let them cool on the pan for a few minutes before moving them, and store them in an airtight container for up to a week, or freeze them for up to a month.

Good to know: Everything is written in American English, and measurements use US customary units — cups, tablespoons, and Fahrenheit — for cooks in the United States. Always follow safe food-handling guidance, and check labels for allergens when cooking for others.

Food safety: the eggs in these cookies are baked through — bake until set and golden. For egg dishes, the USDA safe temperature is 160°F (71°C)..

Classic, nut & cocoa snowballs

The classics that started it all — buttery, nutty rounds rolled twice in powdered sugar until they look like fresh snow.

1. Classic Christmas Snowball Cookies

Buttery, nutty, rolled in sugar.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup finely chopped pecans
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • powdered sugar for rolling

Directions

  1. Cream butter and powdered sugar.
  2. Stir in flour, pecans and vanilla.
  3. Roll into balls and bake at 350°F for 15 minutes.
  4. Roll in powdered sugar while warm, then again when cool.

Buttery caramel, a pinch of salt.

  • 2 cups flour

  • 1 tsp vanilla

  • powdered sugar for rolling

Directions

  1. Cream butter and powdered sugar.

  3. Roll into balls, press a pinch of flaky salt on top, and bake at 350°F for 15 minutes.
  4. Roll in powdered sugar while warm.

Baking & serving tips

Roll the cookies in powdered sugar twice for the snowiest finish, and re-roll them once more before serving if they have been stored. Freeze unbaked dough balls for up to a month, then bake straight from frozen with an extra minute or two in the oven. Mix two or three flavors on one plate for a cookie tray, and keep the cookies in a single layer so the sugar coat stays intact. Leftover powdered sugar keeps well in a jar for the next batch.
